Validation outside Gujarat

With whole regions held out, gradient boosting has the highest F1, {{k:val.f1}} (precision {{k:val.precision}}, recall {{k:val.recall}}), and was selected by the rule. The published threshold rule reaches {{k:val.rule_f1}}.

The way the data is split changes the answer, and not only its level. Under a random pixel split the random forest scores {{k:val.rf_f1_pixel}} and looks like the clear winner. With regions held out it falls to {{k:val.rf_f1}}, below gradient boosting. A project that validated on random pixels would have chosen a different model and reported a number it could not reproduce in a new state.

Bottom line

The model has high recall on dumpsites it is shown: {{k:guj.sites_found}} of {{k:guj.sites}}, plus one site it was never told about at rank {{k:guj.unseen.rank}}. It cannot be used to find new ones without a great deal of manual checking: about one in twenty of its strongest alarms is a dumpsite, and the known sites are a small fraction of one percent of what it flags. The Diagnosis page shows why.
